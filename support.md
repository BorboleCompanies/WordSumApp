## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I play WordSum?
Each day features three puzzles (3, 4, and 5 letters). Every letter has a numerical value (A=1, B=2, ... Z=26). Your goal is to guess the word whose letters add up to the Target Sum.

### How do I start my free trial?
Open the app and tap the subscription button. New users receive a 7-day free trial. After the trial, the subscription renews at $0.99/month.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

**On iPhone/iPad:**
1. Open Settings
2. Tap your Apple ID at the top
3. Tap Subscriptions
4. Select WordSum
5. Tap Cancel Subscription

**On Android:**
1. Open Google Play Store
2. Tap your profile icon
3. Tap Payments and subscriptions
4. Tap Subscriptions
5. Select WordSum
6. Tap Cancel subscription

### My streak reset. Why?
Streaks reset if you don't complete all three daily levels before midnight in your local time zone.

### The app is not loading or is showing an error.
Try closing and reopening the app. If the issue persists, make sure your device is running the latest version of the app and your operating system is up to date.

### How do I restore my subscription on a new device?
Tap Restore Purchase on the subscription screen. This will check for an active subscription linked to your Apple ID or Google account.

### Can I play on multiple devices?
Your subscription is tied to your Apple ID (iOS) or Google account (Android). You can use the app on any device signed in with the same account. Note that game progress is stored locally on each device.
